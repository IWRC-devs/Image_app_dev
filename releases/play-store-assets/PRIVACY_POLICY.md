# Privacy Policy — IWRC Imaging

**Last updated: October 12, 2026**

IWRC Imaging ("the app") is a field data-collection tool built for IWRC weed-imaging research. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

## Summary

**The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.** There is no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no network server that the app communicates with. Everything you capture stays on your device.

## What the app accesses, and why

| Permission | Why it's needed |
|---|---|
| **Camera** | To let you photograph weeds/plants directly within the app. Photos are saved only to your device. |
| **Storage (folder access)** | To save your photos and batch details into a folder you choose on your own device (inside a folder named "IWRC imaging"). The app only writes to this folder — it does not read or access any other files on your device. |

The app requests no other permissions (no location, microphone, contacts, or network access).

## Data storage

Each batch you save — its photos and the associated details you enter (location, plant/site parameters, lighting conditions) — is written as plain files (images plus a text file with the batch details) to the folder you selected when you first saved a batch. This data:

- Stays entirely on your device.
- Is never uploaded to any server, cloud account, or third party, because the app has no network connection to any backend service.
- Can be viewed, copied, or deleted at any time using your device's own file manager.
- Can be deleted from within the app via the "Saved Batches" screen.

## Third parties

The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services. No data is shared with anyone, because none is collected in the first place.

## Children's privacy

The app is a professional field-research tool and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, since it collects no personal data at all.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in the future (for example, if a future version adds an optional sync feature), this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
